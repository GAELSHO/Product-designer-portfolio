## The challenge

<!-- Sustituye este texto por el tuyo. Va como texto, no como imagen: en el
     móvil se reordena solo y siempre se lee al mismo tamaño. -->

Charging an electric car in a public lot is a decision made in seconds, standing
up, often at night. The network needed an identity that reads from across a
parking lot and an interface that answers three questions fast: is it free, how
long will it take, and what will it cost.

## Identity

The mark is a single continuous stroke — a road that rises into a peak and comes
back down. It scales from an app icon to a two-metre column without losing its
shape, and the gradient carries the energy of the brand without needing a
photograph behind it.

![AMPIA charging column installed under a solar canopy, its screen showing the brand mark and the tagline "Powering the future"](./01-charger.webp)

The column is the primary surface. Everything else in the system is built so
that a person who has seen the column recognises the brand anywhere else.

## Campaign

Out of home had to work at 80 km/h. Copy carries the whole idea, the mark closes
it, and the visual does the rest.

![Billboard reading "Tu recibo se pondrá a dieta" with a utility bill wrapped in a measuring tape](./02-billboard.webp)

## Product

The app is the part people touch daily. The icon reuses the same stroke, so the
brand is recognisable in a home screen full of competing colour.

![Close-up of an iPhone home screen showing the AMPIA app icon among system apps](./03-app-icon.webp)
